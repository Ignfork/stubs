The keeper, in plain English.

On every run it re-derives each coin's key, reads both pump.fun vaults (curve and PumpSwap), skips anything under 0.005 SOL, collects the rest, swaps it to USDC and records the payout. If the recipient has linked an account, it's sent right away. If not, it waits safely.

  usdc = jupiter_swap(collect(key))

Questions

Asked at the box office.

Still unsure? The docs explain fees, sweeps and claims in plain language.

Read the docs
Does the person getting paid need crypto?

No. They verify their phone number with a texted code and link the Venmo or Cash App they already have. No wallet, no exchange, no tokens.

Does Stubs take a cut of the fees?

No. Stubs takes nothing from the creator fee. Conversion and deposit costs are shown on each payout.

Can I change the recipient after launch?

No. The recipient is written into the coin when it's minted. Double-check the number or handle before you sign. Fees pointed at a typo can't be claimed.

What if they never claim?

Their payouts wait as "Awaiting payout method" and are released the moment they link an account. The Stub Maker helps you tell them.

Can I launch for someone outside the US?

Yes, you can name anyone. Venmo and Cash App are US-only, so their payouts wait until a supported account is linked.

Stubs, explained.

Launching a coin on pump.fun through Stubs, how its creator fees are tracked, swept and priced, and how the recipient claims them.

Overview

Stubs is a launchpad for pump.fun coins with one change to the money flow: the creator fee a coin earns on every trade is paid in US dollars to the Venmo or Cash App of whoever the launcher names, identified by phone number or username.

  • Launcher: connects a Solana wallet, fills in the coin, signs one transaction and pays the network fee. Never touches the fees.
  • Recipient: the person named at launch. Claims once, then gets paid automatically.
  • Keeper: the scheduled job that collects fees from each coin's vault, swaps them to USDC and deposits dollars.

Launch in two minutes

  1. Open Launch and connect a Solana wallet with enough SOL for Solana network and account rent (about 0.02 SOL) plus any initial buy. pump.fun charges nothing to create a coin.
  2. Upload a square image and enter a name (up to 32 characters) and symbol (up to 10).
  3. Under Fee recipient, pick Venmo or Cash App and enter the person's username or phone number.
  4. Add links and an initial buy if you like. The buy runs in the same transaction as the mint.
  5. Press Launch. Your wallet asks for one signature, and you land on the coin page when it confirms.
🎟️Tell the recipient to visit Claim fees. The Stub Maker makes a card and message for exactly that.

Claiming

  1. Identify: enter your phone number, or link the username the launcher used.
  2. Verify: enter the 6-digit code we text you. Codes expire after a few minutes.
  3. Pick where it goes: a Venmo @username or Cash App $cashtag. You can switch later.

Sweeps that arrive before you link an account wait as "Awaiting payout method" and are released when you link one.

Every coin is a standard pump.fun token: 1,000,000,000 supply, 6 decimals, mint and freeze authority revoked at creation, no presale and no team allocation unless the launcher makes an initial buy.

Fees continue after graduation. Once the coin moves to PumpSwap, swaps there pay the creator fee into a companion vault for the same key, and the keeper sweeps both. Stubs takes nothing from the creator fee.

Sweeps & payouts

The keeper runs on a schedule. For each live coin it re-derives the creator key, reads the vault, skips balances under 0.005 SOL, collects the fees, swaps them to USDC on Jupiter and records a payout. If the recipient has linked an account, it's sent right away. Every sweep and swap is a public Solana transaction.

Security & privacy

Only someone who completes a texted code for the exact number the launcher entered can claim. Public pages show recipients masked, like +1 ••• ••• 0123, and the raw contact never leaves the server. Launch transactions are built server-side and checked before broadcast, so a client can't swap in a different transaction.

Public API

Read-only endpoints. Contacts are always masked.

GET  /api/coins            live coins, newest first
GET  /api/coins/:mint      one coin with fees and claim status
GET  /api/payouts          sweep ledger, masked recipients
GET  /api/analytics        daily fee series and totals
POST /api/launch           build a create (+ buy) transaction
POST /api/claim/start      { phone }        texts a code
POST /api/claim/verify     { phone, code }  returns a session token
POST /api/payout-method    bearer · links Venmo or Cash App

Set CONFIG.API at the top of the page script to your keeper backend and every page reads live from it.
